---
title: "Thel Rebellion"
---

The Thel Rebellion of 998 was not the first time that peasants have risen up to break their chains, but it is the largest in recent history. It started in the early spring of 998, in a small village in the north of the country.

Local peasants formed a militia, forming around the charismatic town leader, Johanne Murk. They took over their Baron's castle while he was away at court, and when he was summoned back and he formed his own army, they fought. The rebels asked for assistance from other nearby villages, laying out their grievances. The other villages joined in the fight.

After their first victory, the militia began spreading slowly South, gathering more peasants and accepting more villages into their midst. For a time, their progress was steady and seemingly unstoppable, until The Bright Queen sent the royal army. Progresss slowed.

In September of 999, the rebellion ended in a final battle. 20,000 rebels faced off against 600 Chosen and the Queen herself. The rebels were killed or captured and now all that remains is small pockets of resistance that are beign systematically hunted down.
